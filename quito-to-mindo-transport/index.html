What determines the final arrangement

Transport is not one universal route. The team needs enough detail to match the vehicle and create a realistic pickup plan.

Pickup and drop-offUse exact locations

Provide the hotel, airport terminal or clear address on both ends instead of only naming the city.

Group and luggageMatch vehicle capacity

Passenger count, suitcases, mobility equipment and camera gear affect the suitable vehicle.

TimingProtect your next commitment

Share flight, check-in, tour or return deadlines so the proposed pickup includes an appropriate margin.

Include these details in your request

A complete first message reduces follow-up and helps the team confirm the right transport format.

  • Travel date and preferred pickup time
  • Exact pickup and drop-off locations
  • Passenger count, including children
  • Number and type of bags or equipment
  • Flight or activity deadlines

Plan travel time with a buffer

Road time between Quito, the airport and Mindo changes with the pickup location, city traffic, weather, road conditions and stops. Avoid building a tight connection around a single estimated duration. The confirmed plan should protect flights, hotel check-in and time-sensitive tours.

Arrival day

Keep the first evening flexible

Flight delays, baggage collection and traffic can shift the arrival. Share live contact details and avoid placing an inflexible activity immediately after transfer.

Departure day

Work backward from the deadline

For flights or fixed appointments, confirm the required arrival time first and let the transport team propose the pickup window.

Transfer or guided day trip?

A transfer moves you between destinations. A day trip combines transport with planned activities and a return schedule. Choose according to whether transportation or the day’s experience is the main need.
