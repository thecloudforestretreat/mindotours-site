What to include

A concise request is enough to start. These details help the team identify realistic options without making assumptions about your schedule, transport or preferred pace.

1

Dates and group

Share your preferred date range, number of travelers and any mobility, age or pacing considerations that affect the day.

2

Main interests

Name the one or two priorities that matter most, such as waterfalls, chocolate, birdwatching, wildlife, adventure or a private mixed plan.

3

Logistics

Mention where you are staying, whether you need pickup or transport and whether you are visiting Mindo from Quito in one day.

Contact questions

These answers explain what happens before a booking is confirmed.

Do I need to know the exact tour before contacting you?

No. Send your dates, group size and priorities. The team can help narrow the options before you choose a final route.

Does submitting a request confirm a booking?

No. The first message starts planning and availability checks. You receive the next step before any booking or payment is treated as confirmed.

Can one request include several activities?

Yes. List your priorities and the team will explain what can fit together and what may require separate timing, transport or specialist coordination.

Can you help with a day trip from Quito?

Yes. Include your pickup area, available time and return constraints so the team can recommend a realistic plan.
